About the project

Why a museum from the future?

Driving question. How can a museum of the future, full of hypothetical artifacts and innovations that helped humanity address the climate crisis, inspire hope today?

Audience

A general audience, so people of all ages and backgrounds can walk in and feel at home.

Methods

An interactive 3D museum online, with exhibits to explore, and a plain page version for every device.

Anti-racist framework

We research places in the world hit hardest by climate change and choose three or four to feature. The wings are regional, so the museum reaches a global scale and centres the people who live there.

Invoking hope

Show a future where the climate crisis was solved by the combined efforts of individuals and communities around the world.
