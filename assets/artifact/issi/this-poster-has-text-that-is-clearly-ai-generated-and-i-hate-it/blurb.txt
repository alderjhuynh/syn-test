Artifact 2: Cook Islands Climate and Indigenous Heritage Treaty
Name: Cook Islands Climate and Indigenous Heritage Treaty
Year: 2063
Origin: Rarotonga, Cook Islands – A fictional international agreement established between the Cook Islands government, Indigenous Cook Islands representatives, traditional leaders, and international climate partners such as New Zealand and Australia.

Description: This treaty signed in 2063 established legal protections for Indigenous lands, cultural heritage, and marine ecosystems across the Cook Islands. It provides funding from New Zealand and Australia for climate adaptation while ensuring Indigenous communities have a central role in environmental decision-making. It also protects traditional knowledge, fishing practices, and culturally significant sites for future generations.
