Name: Treaty Timeline: A Century of Climate Cooperation
Year: 2000–2100
Origin: International Climate Cooperation Archive
Description: This timeline documents the international treaties, agreements, amendments, and climate-related legislation established between 2000 and 2100. It highlights major milestones in global climate cooperation, showing how countries worked together to reduce greenhouse gas emissions, protect ecosystems, advance renewable energy, and promote environmental justice. Visitors can explore key agreements throughout the century and see how international commitments helped shape the world's response to the climate crisis.
