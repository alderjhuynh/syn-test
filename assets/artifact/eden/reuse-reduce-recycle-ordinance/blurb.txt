Name: Reuse, Reduce, Recycle Ordinance

Year: 2042

Origin: Rio De Janeiro, Brazil

Description: A policy designed to reduce urban waste by replacing unnecessary plastics with sustainable, eco-friendly materials throughout the city. The ordinance completely banned single-use products and other disposables. These items were replaced with reusable and recyclable alternatives, successfully reducing landfill waste and halting plastic pollution in the city by over 30% in just six months.
