Name: Two Sides, One Blanket 

Year: 2025

Origin: University of California, San Diego

Description: An educational art project created by Synthesis students at UC San Diego targeting circular fashion and its potential positive impact on the climate crisis. This infinity blanket has two contrasting sides, one using raw and one using upcycled materials to demonstrate the life cycle of clothing. This project raised awareness about fast fashion and overconsumption, showcasing a future for sustainable fashion.
